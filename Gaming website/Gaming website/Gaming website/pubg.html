About PUBG: Battlegrounds

PUBG: Battlegrounds is the pioneer of the battle royale genre, published by Krafton. Drop onto a massive island, scavenge for weapons and equipment, and fight to be the last one standing in this intense 100-player showdown. Inspired by the Japanese film Battle Royale, the game features realistic gunplay, tactical combat, and an ever-shrinking play zone that forces players into increasingly intense encounters.

Squad & Solo Modes
