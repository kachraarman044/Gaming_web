About The Sims

The Sims is the ultimate life simulation game published by Electronic Arts. Create unique characters with distinct personalities, build and decorate dream homes, manage relationships, pursue careers, and live out countless stories in your own virtual world. With extensive expansion packs and a passionate community, The Sims offers infinite possibilities for creative expression.
