About Terraria

Terraria is an action-adventure sandbox game developed by Re-Logic. Dig deep underground to find treasures and resources, fight challenging bosses, explore vast biomes, and build incredible structures. With procedurally generated worlds, hundreds of items to craft, and both single-player and multiplayer modes, Terraria offers hundreds of hours of engaging gameplay.
