About Diablo

Diablo is the iconic action RPG by Blizzard Entertainment that pioneered the hack-and-slash dungeon crawler genre. Descend into dark, randomly generated dungeons, battle demonic hordes, and collect legendary loot to power up your character. With multiple character classes, satisfying combat, and endless replayability, Diablo has defined what action RPGs should be.
