About American Truck Simulator

American Truck Simulator by SCS Software lets you experience the vast landscapes of America from behind the wheel of a massive truck. Haul cargo across iconic highways, explore cities from coast to coast, build your trucking empire, and enjoy the freedom of the open American road. With stunning scenery, realistic physics, and a relaxing gameplay experience, it's the perfect road trip simulator.
