About Guild Wars

Guild Wars is a groundbreaking MMORPG developed by ArenaNet and published by NCSoft. Experience competitive PvP battles, cooperative PvE missions, and explore a vast fantasy world — all without monthly subscription fees. With a deep skill-based combat system, extensive character customization, and a player-driven economy, Guild Wars offers a unique and rewarding online RPG experience.
