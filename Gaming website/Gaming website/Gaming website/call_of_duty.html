About Call of Duty

Call of Duty is a legendary military first-person shooter series published by Activision since 2003. Featuring intense combat, cinematic campaigns, and competitive multiplayer, it remains one of gaming's most iconic franchises. Developed by Infinity Ward, Treyarch, and Sledgehammer Games, the series has evolved through modern warfare, historical battles, and futuristic settings.
