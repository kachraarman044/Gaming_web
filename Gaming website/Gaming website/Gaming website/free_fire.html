About Free Fire

Garena Free Fire is a fast-paced mobile battle royale where 50 players fight on a remote island. With 10-minute matches, diverse characters with unique abilities, and strategic gameplay, it's perfect for mobile gaming. Free Fire has become one of the most downloaded mobile games globally, offering exciting game modes, character customization, and competitive ranked play.
