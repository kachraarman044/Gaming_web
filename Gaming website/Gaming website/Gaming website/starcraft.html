About StarCraft

StarCraft is Blizzard Entertainment's legendary real-time strategy game that defined competitive esports. Command the Terran, Zerg, or Protoss factions in epic battles across the galaxy. With a gripping campaign story, deep strategic gameplay, and one of the most competitive multiplayer scenes in gaming history, StarCraft remains a cornerstone of the RTS genre.
