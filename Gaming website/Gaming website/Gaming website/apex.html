About Apex Legends

Apex Legends is a free-to-play hero shooter battle royale by Respawn Entertainment and published by Electronic Arts. Set in the Titanfall universe, choose from unique Legends, each with powerful abilities, and compete in fast-paced squad-based combat. With cross-platform multiplayer and constant updates, Apex Legends delivers an unmatched competitive experience.
