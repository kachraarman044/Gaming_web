About Fall Guys

Fall Guys is a hilarious multiplayer party game developed by Mediatonic. Race through chaotic obstacle courses, compete in wacky mini-games, and be the last bean standing! With colorful visuals, physics-based gameplay, and endless fun with friends, Fall Guys brings joy and laughter to every gaming session. The game features regular seasons with new courses, costumes, and challenges.
