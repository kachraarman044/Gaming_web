About Euro Truck Simulator

Euro Truck Simulator by SCS Software lets you experience the life of a truck driver across beautifully recreated European landscapes. Deliver cargo across dozens of cities, manage your own fleet, and explore the continent's most iconic highways and countryside roads. With realistic driving physics and a relaxing gameplay loop, it's the ultimate trucking experience.

Realistic Trucking
European Cities
Fleet Management

Truck Customization
