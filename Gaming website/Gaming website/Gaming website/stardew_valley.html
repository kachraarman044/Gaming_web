About Stardew Valley

Stardew Valley is a charming farming simulation RPG created by ConcernedApe. Inherit your grandfather's old farm, grow crops, raise animals, and build relationships with the residents of Pelican Town. Explore caves, fish in rivers, cook recipes, and participate in seasonal festivals in this heartwarming countryside adventure that has captured millions of hearts.
