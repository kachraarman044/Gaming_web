About World of Warcraft

World of Warcraft is the legendary MMORPG by Blizzard Entertainment that defined online gaming. Explore the vast world of Azeroth, battle fearsome foes, complete epic quests, and join millions of players in adventures. With decades of expansions, rich lore, challenging raids, and a vibrant player community, WoW remains the gold standard of MMORPGs.
